The Register

Services

Each appointment opens with a mapping. Nothing is removed before the shape is agreed.

I Brow Mapping The full consultation. Ratio, arch, tail and the asymmetry your face already carries, plotted before anything is touched. 45 min A$55
II Threading A clean, precise edge taken hair by hair. Kinder to fine skin than wax and far more exact. 30 min A$35
III Lamination Direction, not volume. Hairs are set to follow the shape so the brow reads full without a pencil. 75 min A$120
IV Tint Depth matched to your root colour, never darker than your own hair. Softens gaps without drawing them in. 20 min A$25
V Correction For brows grown out, over-plucked or shaped by someone else. We start from what is actually there. 60 min A$105

First visits include the mapping consultation as standard. Patch tests are required forty-eight hours before any tint or lamination.

The Atelier

Nine years, one obsession

Maggie trained in threading before lamination existed as a service, in a room where the only tools were a spool of cotton and a ruler. That order never changed: measure, agree the shape, then remove — and only then reach for anything else.

The studio takes one client at a time. No overlap, no rushing a set to fit a diary. A first appointment runs long on purpose, because the mapping is the part that decides the next six weeks.

“A brow should look like it grew that way. If someone can tell where I stopped, I have taken too much.” Maggie Mac, founder
