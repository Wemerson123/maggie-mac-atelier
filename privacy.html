Privacy policy

Your privacy

Maggie Mac Beauty (“I”, “me”) runs this website at maggiemac.com.au. This page explains what information is collected, why, and what is done with it. Personal information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What is collected

When you book. Appointments are made through Acuity Scheduling. When you book you share your name, contact details and the answers you give in the booking and client forms, which can include skin and health information needed to decide whether a treatment is safe for you. This is used only to prepare for and carry out your treatment and to keep a record of it.

When you message me. If you contact me by WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or phone, I receive the details you choose to share and use them only to answer you.

When you visit the website. Google Analytics shows how many people visit, which parts of the page they look at and which buttons they tap (for example “Book” or “WhatsApp”). It uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. It does not tell me who you are.

How it is used

To book and carry out your treatment, keep basic client records, answer your questions and improve this website. Your information is never sold or rented, and you are not added to marketing lists without asking.

Who it is shared with

Only with the services needed to run the studio — Acuity Scheduling for bookings and payments of booking fees, messaging providers, and Google for website analytics — or where the law requires it.

Cookies and opting out

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The website still works without them.

Access, correction and questions

You can ask to see or correct the information held about you, or raise a privacy concern, by messaging WhatsApp or visiting the studio at Shop 3, 212 David Low Way, Peregian Beach QLD 4573 (inside Nourish Skin Clinic). If you are not satisfied with the response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
